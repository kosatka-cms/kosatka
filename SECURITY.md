# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not report security problems in public issues, discussions or
pull requests.

Report them privately through GitHub: on the repository's
[**Security** tab](https://github.com/kosatka-cms/kosatka/security)
choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Please include:

- what the problem is and what an attacker could do with it;
- the steps to reproduce it (requests, configuration, the version or
  commit);
- whether the problem is already known to others or exploited.

We aim to acknowledge a report within a week. We will keep you informed
while we work on a fix, and we will credit you in the advisory unless you
prefer otherwise. Please give us reasonable time to release a fix before
you disclose the problem.

## Supported versions

Fixes go into the latest release. Please check that the problem exists in
the latest release, or on `main`, before reporting it.

## Scope

This policy covers the engine in this repository: the server, the admin,
the bundled modules and themes. Themes and modules from other
repositories, and the configuration of a particular site, are the
responsibility of their maintainers.
